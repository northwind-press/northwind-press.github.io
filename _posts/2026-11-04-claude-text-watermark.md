Text generated by Claude Mythos 5, Claude Opus 4.7, and Claude Sonnet 4.6 now carries Anthropic's text watermark, according to Anthropic's [API release notes](https://docs.claude.com/en/release-notes/api) for 2026-11-03.

## Which models and platforms

Anthropic's [support article](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16266773-how-claude-marks-ai-generated-content) lists text watermarks on first-party surfaces and on AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Foundry for the Fable 5 and 5.1, Mythos 5 and 5.1, Opus 4.7, 4.8, 5, and 5.5, Sonnet 4.6, 5, and 5.5, and Haiku 5.5 models. For Opus 4.6, Opus 4.5, Sonnet 4.5, and Haiku 4.5, it lists only Content Credentials (C2PA) on files{% include cite.html n=2 %}. On Amazon Bedrock, the rollout for Mythos 5, Opus 4.7, and Sonnet 4.6 completes by 2026-11-10{% include cite.html n=2 %}.

The sources describe the scope differently. Anthropic's [explainer](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-text-watermark), last updated 2026-09-01, opens by saying "Future Claude models" will be watermarked. It adds that the EU law gives models launched before 2026-08-02 a transition period, with watermarking for them rolling out over the coming months{% include cite.html n=4 %}. The support article says Anthropic is adding watermarks to outputs from models released before 2026-08-02{% include cite.html n=2 %}. Neither it nor the release note says when each listed model launched{% include cite.html n=1 %}{% include cite.html n=2 %}.

## What changes, and what doesn't

Anthropic's [developer docs](https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/build-with-claude/text-watermark) say the watermark adds no tokens or hidden characters and carries no information about you or your organization. You don't need to change your requests or response handling{% include cite.html n=3 %}. The explainer says the method is a version of Google DeepMind's SynthID-Text, which changes only the source of randomness used to pick among words{% include cite.html n=4 %}. It says the watermark is sparse in factual passages and code, hard to detect in short texts, and removed by a full rewrite{% include cite.html n=4 %}.

## What to do: the sampling caveat

On Claude Sonnet 4.6, Anthropic's release note says, text generated for a request that sets `temperature`, `top_p`, or `top_k` to a non-default value "might not carry it"{% include cite.html n=1 %}. If you rely on it, the docs say to omit all three, including any that a framework or client library sets for you{% include cite.html n=3 %}. If you deploy Claude in your own product, Anthropic says to assess independently what Article 50 of the EU AI Act requires of you{% include cite.html n=2 %}.

## Who can detect it

Text watermark detection is an API in private preview{% include cite.html n=2 %}. It's open to eligible organizations such as regulators, law enforcement, media, fact-checkers, independent researchers, educational organizations, and EU civil society groups, and to enterprises that must verify watermarking to comply with the Act, through a request form{% include cite.html n=2 %}. The free Claude Content Checker is for files: it checks for Claude-issued Content Credentials{% include cite.html n=2 %}.

## The EU basis

Anthropic says it is watermarking "to comply with the EU AI Act"{% include cite.html n=4 %}. It says it signed the EU AI Act's Article 50(2) Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content{% include cite.html n=2 %}. It says that as of 2026-08-02 the EU requires AI providers serving its market to mark AI-generated content, and that other major developers signed the same Code{% include cite.html n=4 %}. It applies the watermark globally, because it can't yet limit it by region{% include cite.html n=4 %}. We could not read the Act's text on EUR-Lex with our tools, so these EU claims are Anthropic's.
