# About Northwind Press

Northwind Press publishes practical, well-sourced writing on applied AI for working engineers. Accuracy beats speed.

## Sourcing policy {#sourcing-policy}

- Every factual claim links to a source, preferably a primary one: the paper, the official documentation, the release notes, the dataset, or the person who said it.
- Each article ends with a list of the sources it relies on.
- Every article is reviewed by our editor for accuracy before it is published.

## Corrections policy {#corrections-policy}

- When we get something wrong, we fix it and say so. Each correction is noted, with its date, at the top of the article and listed on our [Corrections page](/corrections/).
- We do not silently change published facts.
