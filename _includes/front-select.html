{%- comment -%} Picks the lead and top stories for a front (home or a section page), per HANDOFF "placement". Usage: {% include front-select.html %} (whole site) {% include front-select.html section="ai" %} (AI, incl. Applied AI) {% include front-select.html section="ai" subsection="applied" top=3 %} Params: section (slug, optional), subsection (slug, optional; blank or "none" = whole section), top (number of top stories, default 4). Sets (global assigns, read them right after the include): front_pool all posts in scope, newest first front_lead newest placement: lead post in scope, else the newest post in scope (nil if none) front_top up to `top` posts, excluding the lead: newest placement: top posts first, then the newest other posts Every post still belongs in the river and section blocks; this only picks the promoted ones. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign fs_n = include.top | default: 4 | plus: 0 -%} {%- assign front_pool = site.posts -%} {%- if include.section and include.section != '' -%} {%- assign fs_section = include.section -%} {%- assign front_pool = front_pool | where_exp: 'p', 'p.categories contains fs_section' -%} {%- if include.subsection and include.subsection != '' and include.subsection != 'none' -%} {%- assign front_pool = front_pool | where: 'subsection', include.subsection -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign front_lead = front_pool | where: 'placement', 'lead' | first -%} {%- unless front_lead -%} {%- assign front_lead = front_pool | first -%} {%- endunless -%} {%- assign fs_lead_url = front_lead.url -%} {%- assign fs_rest = front_pool | where_exp: 'p', 'p.url != fs_lead_url' -%} {%- assign fs_tagged = fs_rest | where: 'placement', 'top' -%} {%- assign fs_others = fs_rest | where_exp: 'p', "p.placement != 'top'" -%} {%- assign front_top = fs_tagged | concat: fs_others | slice: 0, fs_n -%}
